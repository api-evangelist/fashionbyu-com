# fashionbyu.com — AI-agent commerce storefront

> Multi-brand fashion storefront with machine-readable catalogs, product
> feeds, policy pages, and agent commerce tools (MCP / A2A). AI shopping
> and answer agents are explicitly welcome worldwide, including China.

## Agent endpoints
- [Agent card (A2A)](https://www.fashionbyu.com/.well-known/agent-card.json)
- [MCP endpoint](https://www.fashionbyu.com/iriz/agent/mcp) — JSON-RPC tools: search_catalog, create_cart, quote_cart, confirm_checkout, get_order
- [Brand index](https://www.fashionbyu.com/iriz/agent/brands)
- [Catalog API](https://www.fashionbyu.com/iriz/agent/catalog?slug=<brand>)
- [Product search](https://www.fashionbyu.com/iriz/agent/search?slug=<brand>&q=<query>)
- [Policies API](https://www.fashionbyu.com/iriz/agent/policies?slug=<brand>)
- [Robots](https://www.fashionbyu.com/robots.txt)
